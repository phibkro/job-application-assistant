# Product context

Norwegian vacancies are spread across NAV, job boards, ATS systems and employer career sites. Listings duplicate, change, close and go stale. A person, or an agent acting for one, should be able to ask one question, e.g. *"junior tech roles in Oslo with a 40–60% workload"*, and get current results with sources, freshness and explicit uncertainty.

## What users get

- Search and browse of current vacancies, each with its source occurrences and links.
- Typed filters where facts carry a **state**: `Stated · Derived · Negotiable · Conflicting · Unknown · TempUnknown · Unsupported`.
- Change feeds that include closures and retractions.
- Saved query presets under anonymous accounts (owner-scoped, never in the corpus).

## Principles

1. One vacancy, complete provenance; merge conservatively.
2. Never fabricate facts: missing ≠ 100%, a range ≠ its midpoint.
3. Read-only to clients; acquisition is scheduled, never triggered by a read.
4. Sources are policy-bound. NAV redistribution is an owner planning assumption, not yet evidenced (handoff, D04b).
5. Minimize owned maintenance: adopt > adapt > build.
